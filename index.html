LIGHTING OUTPUTS

Four outputs, with dual-light control in one room

Outputs 1, 2 and 4 each control one light. Output 3 controls two room lights separately or together through a suitable isolated relay interface.

ELECTRICAL SAFETY

Smart electronics stay on the low-voltage side.

RK LIGHT can control mains-powered lighting, but the ESP electronics are not connected directly to 230V.

IMPORTANT

Use proper isolation for mains lighting.

The controller should operate a correctly rated, isolated switching interface inside a safe enclosure. Installation or changes on the 230V side should be handled by a qualified adult/electrician and follow local electrical requirements.
